Privacy Policy

Asor Notes

Application: com.asornotes.app • Effective Date: September 16, 2026

1. Overview

Asor Notes ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a privacy-first, offline-first personal Bible study, sermon journaling, and scripture concordance application.

Your privacy is our core priority. We believe that your personal reflections, sermon notes, Bible study journals, and prayer items are private. Asor Notes is designed so that your data remains on your device under your complete control.

2. Information We Collect and Store

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any of your personal data.

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of Asor Notes without creating an account or providing your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Notes, Journals & Canvas Drawings: All text entries, formatting, tags, dates, and drawings created within the app are stored exclusively in your device's local memory (IndexedDB / LocalStorage).
  • Audio Recordings: When you record a voice note, the audio file is stored locally on your device. Audio recordings are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party service.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not track unique device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, MAC address) or advertisement identifiers.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

Asor Notes requests only the Android permissions necessary to provide its core features:

  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone): Used solely to record voice notes when you explicitly tap the voice recorder button inside a note. The microphone is never accessed in the background or without your active action. Recordings stay strictly on your device.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used to manage audio playback quality when playing back recorded voice notes through your device's speaker or headphones.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / Storage: Required on supported Android versions to save, play back, and export your recorded voice notes or backup archives.
  • INTERNET: Used exclusively to fetch public Bible scripture text and lexicons if queried online, or to download updates. No telemetry, user logs, or personal information are sent over the network.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Asor Notes does not integrate with third-party tracking services, ad networks, or analytics suites:

  • No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or any advertising SDKs.
  • No behavioral trackers (e.g., Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer).
  • No third-party data brokers.

5. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device:

  • User Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete individual notes, audio recordings, or recently deleted items at any time directly within the app.
  • Clearing Data / Uninstalling: Uninstalling Asor Notes or clearing the application's storage in your device's Android settings will completely erase all local notes and preferences.
  • Export & Backup: You have the ability to export and backup your notes directly to your local file storage at your own discretion.

6. Children's Privacy

Asor Notes does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because the app collects no personal data, it is safe for general audiences of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
